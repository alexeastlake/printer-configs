# Creality CR-PETG - Black

## Info

| | |
|---|---|
| **Material** | PETG |
| **Brand** | Creality (CR series) |
| **Diameter** | 1.75 mm |
| **Color** | Black |

## Notes

- **Best for:** functional/structural parts, things needing heat resistance, weather resistance, or some flex.
- **Bed adhesion warning:** PETG sticks *very* well to smooth PEI. **Always let the bed cool before removing the print** - pulling while warm can take chunks out of the PEI coating.
